OUR MISSION

A place to move. A place to belong.

The CA STRONG Dance Studio Project aims to create accessible dance studios in island and underserved communities where young people have little or no access to safe, dedicated spaces for dance. These studios will serve as community hubs where youth can gather, train, express themselves, develop talent, and build confidence through movement.

“Where Movement Builds Culture.”

We want to bring opportunity closer to communities that are often left out — especially islands and remote areas where young dancers may currently practice in streets, open spaces, or temporary venues.

MOVE · TALK · LISTEN · GROW

Dance can open the door to dialogue.

A strong studio is more than a rehearsal room. It can become a trusted place where young people connect with mentors and peers, talk openly, listen to one another, and build healthier support networks around creativity and movement.

More than a dance studio.

💃

Creative Expression

Youth gain a dedicated environment to dance, explore movement, develop technique, and express their identity through art.

🌿

Mental Wellbeing

Movement and community can support confidence, connection, emotional wellbeing, healthy self-expression, and a stronger sense of belonging.

🌊

Island Opportunity

Studios can create pathways to teaching, workshops, performances, events, and creative entrepreneurship within local communities.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Build the space. Grow the community.

The project is designed to support long-term community participation — not simply build a room and leave. Local dancers and mentors can grow into instructors, organizers, performers, and creative entrepreneurs.

Safe Community Hub

A consistent space for practice, classes, mentoring and positive peer connection.

Youth Leadership

Programs can identify and develop young leaders who can eventually teach and organize locally.

Sustainable Local Economy

Classes, workshops and events can help create income opportunities and keep creative value inside the community.

Care beyond the studio.

Community leadership can extend into service — from feeding programs and volunteer outreach to projects that bring young people together around practical care for neighbors.

Stronger communities in motion.

Movement can inspire action outside the dance floor too: cleaner shared spaces, public art, neighborhood projects and activities that help people feel ownership, pride and belonging.

ARTIST DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM SUPPORT

Develop talent. Build skills. Create opportunities.

Our Artist Development Program Support expands the community mission beyond dance by creating pathways for young and emerging artists to train, collaborate, and grow across multiple creative disciplines.

Support the next generation of artists.

The program is designed to provide practical development, mentorship, creative resources, and access to opportunities for artists working in singing, dancing, music production, and acting. It can support workshops, training sessions, studio access, collaborative projects, showcases, and mentorship led by working creative professionals.
