CORE WORKFLOW

From audio to editable musical data.

The beta is designed to be tested in real production situations. We especially want feedback on transcription accuracy, synchronization, speed, stability and how naturally the workflow fits beside a DAW.

01

Audio → MIDI

Analyze recorded audio and translate musical performance into editable MIDI. Current workflows include keyboard/polyphonic, bass, lead/synth and drum-oriented processing.

02

Precision Piano Roll

Inspect note starts, durations, pitches and velocities against the audio timeline with a smooth playhead, synchronized note/key lighting and smooth follow scrolling.

03

Notation Views

Switch between Piano Roll, Sheet Music, Guitar Tab and Note List. Sheet Music includes horizontal zoom and synchronized playback following.

04

MIDI Timing Strip

Musical position, clock position, BPM, tap tempo, grid/quantize, 4/4 display and a precision-scheduled metronome in one compact transport strip.

05

Keyboard Transport

Computer keyboard control: Space Bar toggles Play/Stop while holding the current position; ← / → rewind or forward 10 seconds.

06

DAW Export

Export converted MIDI for DAW work, including Reason-focused workflows and dedicated Reason 14 KONG drum MIDI mapping.

AUDIO MASTER ENGINE

Mastering is built into the workflow.

Evaluate the sound, not just the interface. The beta includes live processing, metering, interactive EQ and high-quality WAV export so engineers can tell us where the mastering engine needs refinement.

MASTERING

Audio Master Engine

  • Club, Streaming, Spatial, Atmos / Immersive, Binaural, Cinema, Tape / Vintage and Custom modes
  • Interactive 5-band parametric EQ with spectrum display
  • Drive, bus compression, stereo/spatial control and true-peak ceiling
  • Dual VU monitoring, LUFS-I and true-peak readouts
  • A/B comparison and 24-bit PCM WAV export

IMMERSIVE WORKFLOW

Atmos Renderer Handoff Prep

Prepare a structured immersive-audio handoff with 7.1.2 bed/object planning, 48 kHz / 24-bit targets, object rows, frame-rate/downmix choices, QC preparation and renderer handoff packaging.

Important: this beta prepares and visualizes an immersive/Atmos handoff workflow. Final Dolby Atmos rendering, ADM BWF/.atmos creation and certification/QC remain in the licensed Dolby Atmos Renderer or an integrated licensed renderer.
